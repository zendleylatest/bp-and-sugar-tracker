Blood Pressure & Sugar Tracker Knowledge Base

Q: What is Blood Pressure & Sugar Tracker?
A: Blood Pressure & Sugar Tracker is a personal health tracking app for logging blood pressure, blood sugar, BMI, medications, and health reports.

Q: What can users track in the app?
A: Users can track blood pressure readings, glucose readings, BMI data, medication details, reminders, and report history.

Q: Is the app a medical device?
A: No. The app helps users record and understand their own health data, but it does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, or replace professional medical advice.

Q: What should users do if readings are very high, very low, or symptoms are severe?
A: Users should contact a qualified clinician or seek urgent medical care when readings are concerning, symptoms are severe, or they are unsure what to do.

Q: What does Premium unlock?
A: Premium removes ads and unlocks unlimited reports and full data access.

Q: What are the free user limits?
A: Guest users can use 2 actions per limited feature. Signed-in free users can use 5. BMI entries, blood pressure entries, blood glucose entries, reports, and full-data access are tracked separately. Premium users have unlimited access.

Q: What happens when a free user reaches a limit?
A: The app shows a Premium upgrade modal and asks the user to upgrade to continue using the limited feature.

Q: Why should users sign in?
A: Signing in lets the app connect Premium subscriptions to the user's account email and sync access across devices.

Q: Can users continue as guests?
A: Yes. Guests can enter the app, but subscriptions and Premium access require a signed-in account.

Q: How are subscriptions verified?
A: Subscription purchases are verified by the backend against the signed-in user account before Premium is unlocked.

Q: Does Premium remove ads?
A: Yes. Ads are disabled for users with active Premium access.

Q: Where can users see Premium status?
A: Premium status is shown in the app bar and profile with Pro indicators.

Q: Can users restore purchases?
A: Yes. Signed-in users can restore purchases from the Premium screen.

Q: Can the app guarantee health improvements?
A: No. The app supports tracking and reporting, but outcomes depend on the user's health, care plan, and clinician guidance.

Q: Should users change medication based only on the app?
A: No. Users should not change medication, dosage, or treatment plans without advice from a qualified clinician.
